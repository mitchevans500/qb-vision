Read the defense. Make the throw.

Look before the snap. Count the safeties. Then hit the open receiver before the rusher gets to you.

How to read a defense

1Count the safeties standing way back. One, or two?
2Look at the corners on the outside. Up close, or backed off?
3Hike it. Throw to the receiver with nobody near him, before the yellow rusher gets you.

On the field: S is a safety, CB is a corner, LB is a linebacker, and the yellow ! is the rusher. The little white point on each defender shows where he is looking.
